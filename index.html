gtsam-splatfactors

3D Gaussian Splatting SLAM with a factor graph backend: gsplat rendering expressed as GTSAM factors, giving loop closure, multi-sensor fusion, and pose uncertainty that pure gradient-descent 3DGS pipelines lack. Up to 85% ATE reduction on KITTI with loop closure. Written up in 3D Gaussian Splatting meets Factor Graph SLAM on the GTSAM blog.
